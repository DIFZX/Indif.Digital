# InDif Digital sales homepage

Reading this as: a wedding invitation storefront for couples, in a warm editorial visual language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

## Direction

Retain InDif Digital's supplied logo, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, and real invitation mockups. The supplied brief asks for an elegant, modern first impression with looping invitation quotes and visible motion. The approved anti-slop mode is during, for this session only.

References: the installed awesome-design-md collection, Runway's content-led cinematic composition and Oura's serif hierarchy and warm paper surfaces. Adapt these principles to wedding stationery, without copying either brand.

## Decisions and their purpose

- Ink #171614 and ivory #f7f3eb retain the existing dark introduction and make the real invitation artwork stand out; the catalogue uses a light reading surface.
- Champagne #d9bc91 is reserved for the invitation headline and principal action, recalling printed wedding stationery. Social platform colors remain recognizable.
- Georgia's regular and italic forms echo invitation typography without a font download. A system sans keeps navigation and purchasing controls easy to read.
- A composed stack of actual invitation mockups fills the formerly empty hero space. Every preview links to a real, currently active catalogue item.
- The preferred featured themes are Sunda Vibes, Java Vibes, and Pinky Bloom when active. Other active templates fill missing positions; an empty catalogue shows a typographic invitation message, not invented product data.
- The headline, image stack, narrow quote ribbon, catalogue, and compact footer use different compositions to distinguish introduction, emotion, selection, and contact.
- Generous space around the headline gives it emphasis; denser navigation and catalogue controls keep useful actions close together.
- Rounded phone silhouettes echo the real mockups; purchasing controls use restrained corners. Social controls retain circular shapes that distinguish platform shortcuts.
- A shadow only grounds the overlapping mockups. No glow, decorative grid, or generic feature icons are needed.
- A short entrance and gently moving previews direct attention to the actual product. A seamless quote ribbon implements the owner's explicit looping-motion request. The catalogue stays still for comparison.
- The owner explicitly requested removing the pause button, overriding the skill's pause-control requirement for this page. Hover or keyboard focus pauses the preview stack; hovering pauses the quote ribbon. Reduced-motion settings remove nonessential animation entirely.
- The owner's slogan is "Bikin undangan yang kamu banget!", used in the main headline, moving ribbon, motion bar, and footer. A custom invitation service label sits immediately above the WhatsApp consultation link.
- Existing catalogue data fetching, availability, category filters, prices, order URLs, and invitation data remain authoritative. No added statistics, testimonials, or popularity claims.

## Responsive and interaction contract

Narrow phones use stacked copy and previews with all social icons visible. Intermediate widths give the preview its own centered stage. Wide screens use an asymmetric two-column introduction. Verify narrow, intermediate, and wide widths, reduced motion, keyboard focus, actual preview destinations, and the existing catalogue filtering and ordering paths.
